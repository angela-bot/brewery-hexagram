YBYC Regattas

Racing, camaraderie, great food, and a long tradition of welcoming centerboard, keelboat, catamaran, and one-design fleets.

About regattas

A series of races—and everything around them.

Boat racing is an ancient tradition. A regatta is typically a series of races governed by formal rules, with boats grouped by class and accompanied by opportunities for sailors, families, and spectators to gather.

YBYC’s regattas combine competition on Yaquina Bay with skipper meetings, meals, awards, and time together at the clubhouse.

Spring Regatta

The annual Spring Regatta opens the season with racing, camaraderie, dinner, and awards. One or more races constitute a regatta.

Racing is governed by the Racing Rules of Sailing 2025–2028, including US Sailing Prescriptions, and uses the low-point scoring system. If seven or more races are sailed, each vessel’s worst score is excluded.

Season finale

Fall Regatta

The YBYC racing season ends with a Fall Regatta in early October, featuring two days of racing, camaraderie, and food.

The most recent complete archived event took place October 11–12, 2025. Registration included one dinner and one T-shirt, except for club youth sailors.

The information below is retained as a record of the 2025 event. Check the live calendar for current scheduling.
