Youth programs

Young sailors

YBYC supports scholarships, regatta fees, equipment, maintenance, and opportunities for youth sailors. Local classes also run through the Port of Toledo and Oregon Boating Foundation.

Member boats

The club fleet

Qualified members may use six Vanguard Club 420s and the Catalina Capri 22, S/V Fulmar. Checkouts and signed waivers are required.

Wednesday nights

Seasonal race series.

Weeknight racing is free for members and gives both skippers and crew regular time on the water. Results are maintained in live club spreadsheets.

Regattas

Race days on the bay.

The Spring Regatta welcomes centerboard boats, keelboats, catamarans, and qualifying one-design fleets. The club also hosts the Fall Regatta and preserves the history of Bridge to Bridge.
