How to join

Four steps to membership.

Memberships are considered at each monthly board meeting. The membership chair can answer questions and help locate a sponsor.

Contact the membership chair

Ask questions and request help finding a sponsor if needed.

Find a current member sponsor

Every prospective member must be sponsored by a current member in good standing.

Read the club documents

Review the YBYC Bylaws and Members Handbook to understand how the club operates.

Submit application and payment

Send your application, initiation fee if applicable, and dues to the Membership Chair at the clubhouse address.

Member benefits

Plenty of ways to participate.

  • Reciprocal rights at many yacht clubs worldwide
  • Free entry for weeknight sailboat racing
  • Selected club sailboats and powerboats
  • Discounted clubhouse use and sailing classes
  • Crab boat and crab pot access
  • Potlucks, barbecues, programs, and social events
  • Newsletter, volunteer opportunities, and youth sailing support
